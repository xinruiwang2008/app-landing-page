Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Who is Cadmus for?

Cadmus is for curious readers, writers, students, English learners, and anyone who wants to build a richer personal vocabulary.

What language does Cadmus support?

Cadmus is launching as an English vocabulary learning app.

Do I need an account?

Yes. An account keeps your saved words, collections, notes, and learning progress connected to you.

Can I use Cadmus offline?

Cadmus requires an internet connection at launch. We are exploring thoughtful offline access for a future release.

How do I delete my account?

You can request account deletion from Profile settings in the app. For help, visit our support page.
