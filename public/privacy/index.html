Cadmus Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Please note: this policy is subject to legal review before publication.

This Privacy Policy explains how Omnisciento LLC ("Cadmus," "we," "us," and "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the Cadmus mobile application, website, and related services (the "Service").

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

When you create or use an account, we collect:

  • your email address and account identifier;
  • your display name, if you choose to provide or change one;
  • authentication and account-status information, including email-verification status; and
  • content you create in the Service, such as saved words, collections, learning progress, familiarity settings, and notes.

Cadmus uses AWS Cognito to provide account registration, sign-in, email verification, password recovery, and session management. We do not receive or store your account password in our application database.

Information generated through use

We collect information needed to operate and improve the Service, including:

  • account activity such as searches, saved words, collections, practice activity, and profile activity;
  • limited product analytics such as feature use, search result counts, selected word identifiers, and app version; and
  • zero-result search terms, so we can identify missing dictionary coverage.

We do not intentionally send note text, access tokens, passwords, or raw search terms to Firebase. Zero-result search terms are sent to Cadmus's backend only for content-coverage purposes.

Device and technical information

When you use the Service, our infrastructure and service providers may process technical information such as IP address, device and operating-system details, app version, network and request logs, and security information. This information is used for delivery, security, troubleshooting, and preventing abuse.

If Firebase is enabled in the version of the app you use, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may collect device, app-interaction, and diagnostic information under their service terms and privacy practices. Cadmus uses these services for product analytics and crash diagnosis, not for advertising or sale of personal information.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • provide, personalize, maintain, and secure the Service;
  • authenticate users and synchronize account-dependent data across devices;
  • provide search, saved-word, collection, note, and learning-practice features;
  • deliver illustrations and other media;
  • understand feature use, improve content coverage, and fix errors;
  • communicate about your account, security, support, or legal notices; and
  • comply with law, enforce our Terms, and protect users and Cadmus.

3. How we disclose information

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We may disclose information to service providers that process it for us and only under instructions consistent with this Policy, including:

  • Amazon Web Services: Cognito for identity services; RDS for application data; EC2 for backend processing; and private S3 with CloudFront for media delivery.
  • Firebase, if enabled: Analytics and Crashlytics for product analytics and crash reporting.
  • Apple: when you download or use the iOS app, Apple may process data under its own privacy policy.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect rights and safety, investigate fraud or security issues, or as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or asset sale.

4. Data retention and deletion

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide the Service and for the purposes described in this Policy. Retention periods can vary by data type, operational need, and legal obligations.

You can request account deletion in the app's Profile settings. On completion, we delete Cadmus account data and request deletion of the associated Cognito account. We may retain limited information where necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, backup, or enforcement purposes. Before publication, specify the actual backup-retention period and any analytics or log-retention periods.

5. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. Cadmus uses HTTPS for app-to-service traffic; uses Cognito access tokens for authenticated requests; keeps application data in AWS RDS behind the backend service; and uses private S3 origins with CloudFront for media delivery.

No system is completely secure. Please use a unique password and protect access to your device and email account.

6. Your choices and rights

You can update your display name and request account deletion from Profile settings. You can stop using the Service at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about our processing of your personal information. To make a request, email info@cadmusapp.com from the email address associated with your account. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights provided by applicable law. Counsel must confirm whether Cadmus meets the thresholds or has obligations under the California Consumer Privacy Act and other applicable state privacy laws, and add any required state-specific disclosures.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact info@cadmusapp.com so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. International users

Cadmus is intended for users in the United States. If you access the Service from outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States and other locations where our service providers operate.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes to the Service, our practices, or applicable law. If we make a material change, we will provide reasonable notice through the Service, by email, or by another lawful method. The updated Policy will identify its effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at info@cadmusapp.com.
