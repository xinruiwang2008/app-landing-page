Frequently asked questions

Learn how Cadmus helps you build your English vocabulary.

Is Cadmus free to use?

Yes. Cadmus is free to download and includes unlimited word searches, visual practice, Favorites, and a personal collection. The free plan limits saved words, collections, and new practice sessions. An optional Plus subscription offers more.

What do I get with Cadmus Plus?

Plus offers unlimited personal collections and saved words, access to more curated collections, and unlimited practice sessions. Prices and subscription terms are shown in the app before you subscribe.

Where can I download Cadmus?

Cadmus is available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store.

How does vocabulary practice work?

Practice uses short questions about words, meanings, and illustrations. Cadmus uses spaced repetition: it adjusts your mastery level based on practice and changes how often words come back for review.

How can I use Cadmus to build my English vocabulary?

Look up unfamiliar words and explore their meanings, illustrations, pronunciation, and examples. Save words to Favorites or a collection, add notes, and practice them. Discover more words through Daily Word and curated Cadmus collections.

Can I use Cadmus to prepare for vocabulary tests?

Yes. Explore curated test-prep collections or save words from your study materials to your own collection. Practice uses spaced repetition and tracks your mastery as you learn. Cadmus can complement your preparation, but it is not an official exam guide and does not guarantee a score.

Is Cadmus useful for English language learners?

Yes. Explore English words through pictures, definitions, pronunciation, and examples, and save useful words to your own collections.
