Quick help

Start here.

Sign-in or verification

Check your spam folder for the verification email and confirm that you entered the same email address used to create your account. You can request a new code from the app.

Reset your password

Choose “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen and follow the instructions sent to your account email.

Report word content

Use the feedback option on a word page to report an illustration, meaning, or example. Add a short note when context would help our editorial team.

Delete your account

In Cadmus, open Profile, go to Settings, and choose Delete Account. If you cannot access the app, email us from the address associated with your account.

Reporting a problem

A little context helps.

When contacting support, describe what you expected and what happened. If possible, include your Cadmus app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and a screenshot that does not reveal private information.
