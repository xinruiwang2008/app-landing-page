Quick help

Start here.

Sign-in or verification

Check your spam folder for the verification email and make sure you entered the email address used to create your account. You can request a new code in the app.

Reset your password

Choose “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen and follow the instructions sent to your account email.

Report word content

Use the feedback option on a word page to report a problem with an illustration, meaning, or example. Add a note if it helps explain the issue.

Delete your account

In Cadmus, open Profile, go to Settings, and choose Delete Account. If you cannot access the app, email us from the address associated with your account.

Reporting a problem

Tell us what happened.

Describe what you expected and what happened instead. If possible, include your app version, device model, iOS version, and a screenshot without private information.
