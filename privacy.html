Privacy policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Our approach

This Privacy Policy explains how Omnisciento LLC (“Omnisciento,” “we,” “us,” and “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Cadmus mobile application, website, or related services (the “Service”).

Information we collect

Information you provide

When you create or use a Cadmus account, we may collect your email address, account identifier, optional display name, and content you create in the Service, such as saved words, collections, learning progress, and notes.

Cadmus uses Amazon Cognito for registration, sign-in, email verification, password recovery, and session management. We do not store your account password in our application database.

Information generated through use

We collect information needed to operate and improve the Service, including searches, saved words, collections, practice activity, profile activity, feature use, selected word identifiers, app version, and zero-result searches that help us improve content coverage.

Device and technical information

Our infrastructure and service providers may process IP address, device and operating-system details, app version, network and request logs, and security information for delivery, security, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention.

How we use information

  • Provide, personalize, maintain, and secure the Service.
  • Authenticate users and synchronize account data.
  • Provide search, saved-word, collection, and learning features.
  • Understand product use, improve content, and fix errors.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate legal notices.
  • Comply with law and protect users and the Service.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We may share information with service providers that process it on our behalf, including:

  • Amazon Web Services for identity services, application hosting, databases, and private media delivery.
  • Firebase, if enabled for product analytics and crash diagnosis.
  • Apple when you download or use the iOS app, subject to Apple’s privacy practices.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect rights and safety, investigate fraud or security issues, or support a merger, acquisition, financing, or asset sale.

Retention, choices, and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Service and for legal, security, backup, and operational purposes. You can update your display name and request account deletion in the Cadmus app’s Profile settings. You may also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information.

Security and children

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, including HTTPS, authenticated access tokens, and protected backend infrastructure. No system is completely secure.

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us.

Changes and contact

We may update this Policy as the Service or applicable law changes. Material changes will be communicated through the Service, by email, or by another lawful method. The updated Policy will show its effective date.

For privacy questions or requests, contact info@cadmusapp.com.
